pumper-revere.glb — derivative of pumper.glb (CC0): recolored materials (Revere-style red, white stripe,
red wheels) plus simple "REVERE"/door decal quads, approximating Revere MA E-One pumpers seen in
Greenwood Emergency Vehicles' Sept 2026 delivery photos. Not CAD of the actual apparatus. (v4.48)

v4.49: pumper-revere.glb rebuilt from pumper.glb (CC0): passenger-side pump panel/gauges/valves REMOVED
(driver-side only, matching Revere's E-One Typhoons), brushed-stainless driver pump panel + Darley 1250 GPM label,
red/yellow rear chevrons, black Line-X steps, red/white warning lenses, E-ONE front badge, lower white stripe.
Reference: greenwoodev.com/revere-ma-two-2-e-one-custom-pumpers/ (12 delivery photos), revere.org news 2026-09-14.
GLBs quantized + WebP textures (gltf-transform). Unused raw pumper.glb no longer shipped. Approximation, not CAD.

v4.64: Engine 1 only is replaced by a photogrammetry scan, models/engine1/engine1.glb
(Draco, KHR_draco_mesh_compression, decoder vendored at vendor/draco/1.5.6/ for model-viewer 3.5.0).
The supplied file was engine1-web-draco.glb, sha256 754cd8f6f83009f1c4b72e7b2d5f9ebac46634cb9500eb229b2086141ccd2184,
1,606,521 triangles. Geometry bytes are unchanged. The 4096 JPEG texture was re-encoded
(ffmpeg q:v 3, SSIM 0.990, PSNR 45.6 dB) so the static folder fits a 25MB host, and
KHR_materials_unlit was added so the baked photo is not relit. No triangles were removed.
Ground and background scan artifacts are still in the mesh. Compartment marker positions
are approximate, from visual inspection, not a survey. The old stand-in models/pumper-revere-e1.glb
was removed. models/pumper-revere.glb was removed because it was byte-identical to
models/pumper-revere-e3.glb; the viewer fallback now points at the Engine 3 file.
Engines 2–5 and both ladders are the same stand-in GLBs as v4.63.
